# Listing fixture

## Working title
Personalised Pet Birthday Roast Card + Video | Funny Custom Card From Your Dog or Cat

## Core promise
Upload four photos of your pet and give us the gossip. We turn your pet into a comedy performer,
print a personalised birthday card, and add a private QR reveal so the recipient can watch the pet roast them.

## Customer-facing photo instructions
**Please upload the same pet in all four slots.**

- Front face — eyes visible, ears/head not cropped
- Full body — all four legs/body shape visible if possible
- Side view — useful for building the avatar
- Favourite photo — personality/reference

Best results: daylight, sharp image, no filters or stickers. Phone photos are fine.

## Product template strategy
The card design shown in this listing is the design the buyer receives. The buyer personalizes the **content**,
not the layout. This avoids another decision and makes the generation/print pipeline deterministic.
