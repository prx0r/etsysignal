# Etsy Sales Data Analysis

## From 100-Shop Study (100K+ sales each)

### Revenue Drivers
1. **Volume** — 1,000+ listings, 5+ products, 10 photos each
2. **Sales urgency** — 25%+ off, countdown timers
3. **Pricing strategy** — low-price variants, shipping under $6
4. **Personalization** — 66% of top sellers customize
5. **SEO** — 130+ chars, all 13 tags, long-tail keywords
6. **Professional photos** — 91% have professional mockups

### Key Stats
- 93/100 run sales (25%+ off)
- 81% charge for shipping (under $6)
- 74% have 500+ listings
- 66% do personalization
- 74% use 130+ char titles
- 96% use all 13 tags

### Conversion Factors
- Personalization searches up 43% YoY
- 1 in 3 buyers comes for personalized items
- Flatlay mockups win on top-selling items
- 49/100 have listing video

## Revenue Model

- Digital product = infinite margin
- $5/day agent budget
- No shipping, no inventory
- Seasonal peaks: Father's Day, birthdays, Christmas
- Personalization premium: +$5-15 per item
