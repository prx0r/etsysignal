# Etsy Market Analysis

## Key Metrics (from 100-shop study)

### Pricing
- 81% charge for shipping (19% free)
- 67% charge under $6 (Etsy prioritizes)
- Median shipping: $5.70
- 59% use low-price variants to appear cheaper

### Listings
- 74% have 500+ listings
- 60% have 1,000+ listings
- Median: 1,611 listings
- 97% offer 2+ products

### SEO
- 74% use 130+ chars in title
- 96% use all 13 tags
- 73% separate keywords with comma+space

### Personalization
- 66/100 top sellers have customized products
- 1 in 3 buyers comes for personalized items
- Personalization searches up 43% YoY

### Photos
- 91/100 have professional photos
- 48/100 use all 10 listing photos
- 49/100 have listing video

## Revenue Potential

- Digital product = infinite margin
- $5/day agent budget covers generation
- No shipping, no inventory
- Seasonal peaks: Father's Day, birthdays, Christmas
