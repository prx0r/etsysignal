# Roast Script Template

## Structure

### 1. Intro (3-5 sec)
"Hello {recipient_name}. It's me, {pet_name}."

### 2. Premise (5-8 sec)
"Apparently I've been asked to say something nice for your {occasion}."
OR
"So {buyer_name} hired me to tell you the truth."

### 3. Roast Beats (15-30 sec)
4-6 personalized jokes from funny_facts:
- "{fact_1}... honestly, how do you live with yourself?"
- "{fact_2}... I've seen dogs with better taste"
- "{fact_3}... and you call yourself an adult?"

### 4. Affection Turn (5-8 sec)
"But honestly, {recipient_name}... you do give decent treats."
OR
"But I wouldn't trade you for all the kibble in the world."

### 5. Signoff (3-5 sec)
"Happy birthday, you old fraud. Love, {pet_name}."

## Tone Variants

### light_cheeky
- Gentle teasing
- Wholesome ending
- Family-friendly

### funny_cheeky
- Medium roasting
- Some edge
- Still warm

### savage_safe
- Hard roasting
- Sharp jokes
- Always affectionate turn at end
