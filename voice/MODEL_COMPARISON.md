## Recommendation

**Google Gemini 3.8 Live** for Game Winner:
- 10x cheaper ($0.005/min vs $0.05/min)
- 97 languages (international Etsy sales)
- Visual context (show video previews)
- Async function calling (process payments while talking)
- Partners: LiveKit, Pipecat (easy integration)

## Cost Estimate

- 1,000 voice interactions/month
- Average 2 minutes each
- Gemini 3.8: $0.005 × 2min × 1000 = $10/month
- GPT-Live-1: $0.05 × 2min × 1000 = $100/month

**Gemini saves $90/month at scale.**
