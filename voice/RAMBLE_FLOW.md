# Ramble Flow — Voice Commerce via Google/ChatGPT Live Models

## The Opportunity

Google just dropped intelligent live models. ChatGPT has Advanced Voice Mode.
Neither has a commerce integration. We can be first.

## How It Works

1. Customer opens Google/ChatGPT voice mode
2. Says "I want a personalized football video for my dad's 50th"
3. Agent understands intent, asks: "What's his name? What team does he support?"
4. Agent generates video concept, shows preview image
5. Customer says "I love it, buy it"
6. Agent processes payment via Etsy API
7. Agent says "Done! Your video will be ready in 24 hours"

## Why This Wins

- **Zero friction** — voice is faster than typing
- **Emotional** — voice = trust = higher conversion
- **Personal** — "tell me about your dad" = custom video
- **First mover** — no one else is doing voice commerce on Etsy
- **AI-native** — built for the voice-first future

## Implementation

### Phase 1: Voice Customer Service
- Voice agent handles Etsy inquiries
- "Where's my order?" → instant answer
- "Can I get a refund?" → automated process

### Phase 2: Voice Product Discovery
- "Show me football gifts for dad"
- Agent shows relevant listings
- "I like that one, tell me more"

### Phase 3: Voice Purchase
- "Buy that one"
- Agent processes payment
- "Done! Video will be ready in 24 hours"

## Tech Stack

- Google Gemini Live (just dropped)
- ChatGPT Advanced Voice Mode
- ElevenLabs for custom voices
- Etsy API for listings/payments
- Webhook for order notifications
