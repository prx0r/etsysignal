# Voice Commerce — The Next Frontier

## Why Now

Google just dropped intelligent live models. ChatGPT has voice mode.
The intersection of voice AI + commerce is wide open.

## The Flow

1. Customer says "I want a personalized football video for my dad"
2. Voice agent understands intent, asks clarifying questions
3. Agent generates video concept, shows preview
4. Customer approves, pays via voice
5. Agent delivers digital product

## Why It Works

- **Zero friction** — no typing, no browsing
- **Emotional** — voice = trust = higher conversion
- **Personal** — "tell me about your dad" → custom video
- **Instant** — AI generates in real-time

## Tech Stack

- Google Gemini Live (just dropped)
- ChatGPT Advanced Voice Mode
- ElevenLabs for narration
- Runway/H3 for video generation
- Etsy API for fulfillment

## The Play

1. Set up Game Winner shop
2. Add voice agent for customer service
3. Expand to voice-first shopping
4. Own "personalized video gifts via voice"
